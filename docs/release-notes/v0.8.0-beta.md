# v0.8.0-beta (draft)

Portrait-first Android build of Slay the Spire 2. The whole run reads and
plays in one hand: bar, map, combat, rewards, events, shops, rest sites, the
timeline and the run-end screens have a phone composition, and the layout
derives from the canvas so shorter phones (16:9) keep the same rules.

## What changed since v0.3.0

- Main menu, profile, patch notes: thumb-sized corner buttons that take a
  tap, rows that fit the band under the logo on any height, back tabs at
  the top of every submenu.
- Combat: touch-friendly hand and End Turn, potion capsule at its bar
  station outside combat, creature hitboxes grown to a touch minimum, no
  stray selection brackets on touch.
- Map: bottom row reaches the bottom of the screen, parchment covers the
  view above the boss, pinch to zoom, furniture yields to pause and
  settings.
- Rewards and picks: 2+1 card grid at thumb size, loot panels that grow
  with the reward count, Skip on a stable plate, grid screens (deck view,
  smith, remove) with one footer strip.
- Events and ancients: wrapped option plates sized to their text, scenes
  framed per ancient (Neow, Tezcatara, Tanx), Crystal Sphere divination
  laid out for portrait.
- Shops, rest sites, treasure: shelves and pitch derived from the panel
  height, closed shop panel fully off screen, plates on the footer strip.
- Timeline, unlocks, game over: intro text wrapped, epoch row at thumb
  size, unlock notices and run-end plates on the strip, compact bar on the
  summary.
- Confirmation modals at 1.5x.

## Known limits

- Audio needs your own licensed libfmod.so; the build ships without it.
- Tested on one 1440x3168 device; 16:9 layouts were checked through the
  in-app emulation, not on a real 16:9 phone.
- A game update can move nodes the patches look up by name; see
  docs/BUGS.md for the patch rules.

## Build

`scripts\build-android-local.ps1` with JDK 17, Android SDK 35, NDK
28.1.13356709, Gradle 8.11.1 (see docs/TESTS.md, "Build environment").
